02 Data handling & retention

Your documents stay yours — and stay deletable.

Client documents are encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3, TLS 1.2 floor) and at rest (AES-256, customer-managed keys via Cloud KMS, rotated every 90 days). Tenants are isolated at two independent layers — application RBAC and database row-level security — so cross-tenant access is structurally impossible, not merely forbidden.

  • Right to erasure is engineering, not a promise: a purge pipeline removes vector, relational and object-storage records in one operation.
  • Backups are point-in-time-recoverable and stored dual-region — inside the EEA.
  • Every user action, AI verdict and human override is written to an append-only, hash-chained audit log.

03 AI & model governance

No AI verdict is final without a human.

Audomate's analytical engine is retrieval-augmented generation: every answer is grounded in retrieved fragments of your own documents and the regulation itself, returned with citations and a confidence score. A compliance officer reviews, edits or overrides every verdict — with author, timestamp and reason recorded. The AI proposes; your team decides.

  • LLM inference runs on enterprise EEA endpoints under a contractual zero-data-retention policy — prompts and completions are not stored by the model provider and are never used for training, ours or theirs.
  • Each tenant's audit session is fully isolated; context is held in memory for the session and discarded at its end.
  • Prompt-injection detection and input sanitisation run at the gateway, before any content reaches a model.
  • Token budgets are enforced per tenant with utilisation alerts — no runaway processing.
  • Organisations requiring complete independence from third-party model providers can run Audomate with an on-premise model stack (Enterprise plan).

04 Organisational & platform security

Boring, layered, verifiable.

  • Network — WAF with OWASP Top-10 protection and adaptive DDoS mitigation in front of the API; mTLS between internal services; data layer on private networking with no public IPs.
  • Identity — SSO via SAML 2.0 / OIDC with your identity provider (e.g. Microsoft Entra ID); MFA mandatory; 15-minute access-token TTL with refresh rotation.
  • Access — role-based access at application level, enforced a second time by database row-level security; secrets in a managed vault with access audit and 90-day rotation.
  • People — the platform is built by a team whose experts advise the EU Commission on Digital Identity Wallets, sit on CEN and PKN standardisation committees, and consult the Polish Bank Association on AML, eIDAS and PSD.
  • Development — TO CONFIRM: code review policy, dependency scanning, release testing practice — one sentence each from engineering.

06 GDPR / RODO

Processor by contract, minimalist by design.

  • For client documents, your organisation is the data controller and DeepTech Sp. z o.o. is the processor. A Data Processing Agreement with technical-and-organisational-measures and subprocessor annexes is signed as part of every deployment.
  • No personal data is transferred outside the EEA, so no SCCs or adequacy mechanisms are relied on for platform operation.
  • Personal-data breaches are notified to affected clients without undue delay, and to the supervisory authority within 72 hours where required by art. 33 GDPR.
  • Data-subject rights — access, rectification, erasure, restriction — are supported in the platform, including hard deletion across all storage layers.
  • A record of processing activities (ROPA) is maintained internally and available for review under NDA.
