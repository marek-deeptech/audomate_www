Incident Response & Breach Notification Summary

DeepTech Sp. z o.o. · Draft v0.9 · September 2026

How DeepTech detects, escalates and communicates incidents affecting the Audomate platform.

1. Severity model and response

SeverityResponseEscalation & notification
Critical (P1) — security or integrity of a tenant affectedImmediate tenant isolation; automatic shutdown of the affected tenant if required; on-call engineer pagedCTO + CEO escalation within 15 min; affected-client notification within 30 minutes (Enterprise) or 24 hours (Standard/Pro); RTO 1 h for isolation actions
High (P2) — degraded function, no data exposureRoot-cause analysis within 2 h; patch or workaround deployed; tracked in ticketingTeam-lead escalation; written report within 24 h
Personal-data breachDedicated Data Breach Response Plan; privacy lead role holder to confirm and legal engaged immediatelySupervisory authority within 72 h where required (art. 33 GDPR); affected clients without undue delay, no later than 24 hours; data subjects where art. 34 applies

2. Detection

Centralised monitoring and logging with error-rate, latency and anomaly dashboards; identity-provider anomaly detection; WAF and DDoS telemetry; client SIEM forwarding available (syslog / REST).

3. Communication

  • Named client contacts recorded at onboarding receive incident notifications primary channel (e-mail / phone) to confirm.
  • Written incident report within 24 h (P1/P2) including scope, impact and remediation.
  • Post-incident review within 5 working days for P1.
  • Public status page: status.audomate.eu — in setup; will be linked here when live.

4. DORA alignment

For clients in scope of DORA, contractual notification supports the client’s own incident-classification and reporting duties; incident records are retained in the hash-chained audit trail (minimum 1 year, DORA art. 25 context).
