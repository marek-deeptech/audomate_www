2. What we collect, why, and for how long

Demo and pilot enquiries. When you submit the demo / pilot form we process your name, business e-mail address, company name and the regulatory frameworks you indicate. We use this to respond to your enquiry, arrange the demo or pilot, and follow up on it (art. 6(1)(b) GDPR — steps prior to entering a contract, and art. 6(1)(f) — our legitimate interest in handling business enquiries). We retain enquiry data for 24 months from our last contact with you, unless a contract is concluded.

Direct contact. If you write to us or call, we process the contact details and content you provide, to handle the matter (art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Correspondence is retained as long as needed for the matter and any related claims.

Technical server data. Our hosting infrastructure records standard technical logs (IP address, request time, user agent) for security and reliability (art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Logs are kept short-term and are not used to profile visitors.

Audomate platform users. Data processed inside the Audomate platform (accounts, uploaded documents) is processed on behalf of your organisation as controller, under a Data Processing Agreement. Details, including our subprocessors and data-residency commitments, are published in the Trust Center.

3. Cookies

This website is designed to work without tracking. We do not use advertising cookies, and we do not sell or share personal data for marketing purposes.

  • Strictly necessary storage — used only where required for the website or the Trust Center access gate to function (e.g. session or security tokens). These do not require consent under art. 173 of the Polish Telecommunications Law.
  • Analytics and marketing cookies — none are in use. If we introduce analytics in the future, this policy will be updated first and any non-essential cookies will be subject to consent.
  • Third-party content — fonts are served from Google Fonts; the font request discloses your IP address to the provider as a technical necessity of delivery.
